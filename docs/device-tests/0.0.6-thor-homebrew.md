## Touch, audio and lifecycle follow-up

The owner reports seeing and interacting with the smaller guest view on the
top physical screen. Nevertheless, the saved session records **zero guest touch
updates**. The four Android callbacks cannot establish valid touch-down positions:
this report does not distinguish an in-image press from a release or a touch
outside the letterboxed image. Input clearing on pause/focus loss does not
increment that callback counter. Brief-input consumption and its frame-boundary
observation also need checking. The source and report do not yet establish which
explanation applies; do not claim either that touch is broken or that its measured
gate passed.

Audio was explicitly confirmed audible. Android generated 2,305,120 guest PCM
frames, converted/wrote 3,380,715 output frames and measured 3,379,585 played
frames. It recorded **zero queue drops**, 1,130 flushed frames, 423 partial writes
and **197 underruns**. Guest PCM covers about **70.432 seconds**, compared with
71.467 seconds foreground time; measured playback covers about 70.408 seconds.
These values warrant a pacing/startup-buffer review. They neither establish the
duration or audible severity of each interruption nor benchmark a retail game.

The evidence supports pause/resume progress and normal host close. Activity/view
replacement was not tested. Preserve that gap rather than inferring recreation
from the pause/resume counters.

The correction/retest should make the one-display layout explicit, distinguish
valid lower-view touch from outside/release events, reconcile actual guest touch
consumption, and review frame/audio pacing. The focused device check should hold
and drag inside the lower guest image, release, confirm both views and sound,
exercise background/resume and view recreation where available, and close/save
normally. Keep untested behavior explicit. **0.0.7 remains gated** on this
interactive qualification; no private title is qualified by this report.
